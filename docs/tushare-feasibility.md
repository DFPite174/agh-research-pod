# Tushare Pro feasibility — CEQ 2025 Chinese convertible-bond paper

Official-document audit date: 2026-11-03. No provider login, token or API data probe was available. Parent confirmed no credential environment variables. No purchase, account modification, target-repository code edits or secret retrieval occurred. This is documentation feasibility, not empirical coverage acceptance.

## Decision

Paid Tushare is a credible candidate for much of the market/contract input collection, including historical and delisted bonds; it is not justified to promise that a base paid account alone exactly reproduces the paper's 696 bonds / 14,980 monthly observations. Additional independent permissions exist, especially conversion-price history and Chinese treasury spot curves. Exact early coverage, clauses, coupon/terminal cashflows and author risk-factor series remain to be verified.

Do not state that Tushare lacks historical conversion changes, clause text or spot curves: the official endpoints explicitly offer all three. Equally, endpoint fields and the words 全部历史 do not prove completeness or vintage fidelity for the paper's sample.

## Paper inputs that must be preserved

Parent's full-paper visual inspection: sample2000-07..2022-06, monthly monthend observations, 696 bonds /14,980 bond-months; five models BS,TF,LYW,LW,ZL. Pricing riskfree rate comes from historical **Chinese treasury spot term structure**, interpolated to remaining maturity. Paper credit-spread anchors (Zheng–Lin2003) are0.65%,0.72%,0.90%,0.98% at1month,1year,3years,5years; do not substitute Tushare's pure-bond-value-implied spread or contemporary ratings. Portfolio excess-return riskfree series is **Chinese one-year deposit rate**, distinct from pricing spot rates. FF5/Carhart factors come from CSMAR; HXZ4/SY4/DHS3 from FactorWar, cited access2022-08-06. Their exact series are a separate supplier requirement.

## Current official quoted access costs, not purchase guarantee

Source [https://tushare.pro/document/1?doc_id=290](https://tushare.pro/document/1?doc_id=290) checked2026-11-03. It explicitly distinguishes points-based endpoints from separately granted endpoints; points are thresholds rather than consumable credits (doc108).

- Personal≥2000points tier:200RMB/year,200requests/minute,100000calls/API/day.
- Personal≥5000points tier:500RMB/year,500requests/minute,normal-data total unlimited; needed for cb_rate/cb_call. Endpoint row caps still apply.
- Conversion-price history separate permission:500RMB/year personal, advertised full history. Thus baseline5000tier+conversion-history quoted total is1000RMB/year personal **before** yc_cb or announcement access. This is an indicative access configuration, not a promise of paper completeness.
- Announcement permission:1000RMB/year personal. With prior two items,2000RMB/year personal before yc_cb. Permission table says10+years while anns_d endpoint says start1999-01-01; exact eligible dataset and old PDF coverage should be confirmed.
- yc_cb says contact administrator for separate permission. No verified public price or earliest-date promise; do not invent one or assume part of the5000tier.
- Official table says corporate/institution pricing10×personal; verify actual account classification/quote. Do not present personal rate as organization license.
- Extra2000RMB/year factor library is not a justified purchase for obtaining the paper's five factor-return models; its documented content differs.

## Included qualification adapter

The explicit `probe_tushare()` adapter uses the reviewable [probe manifest](../configs/tushare-probes.json). It performs no requests until called with `TUSHARE_TOKEN` configured privately. Outputs contain request names, row counts, dates and outcome categories; raw rows, provider error bodies and credentials are omitted. A small successful probe remains `QUALIFICATION_INCOMPLETE` and never marks paper coverage verified.

HTTPS transport was checked without credentials on 2026-11-03: the public [API endpoint](https://api.tushare.pro) returned its JSON malformed-request response over HTTPS. This verifies HTTPS endpoint availability, not account entitlement or historical data coverage.
